# Curated scene cards and future personal recommendations

## Available in the frontend prototype

The character scene keeps its translucent blurred article card, with readable text and a quiet direction arrow instead of the bracket ornament. An accompanying series card uses the same visual language. Studio's character-scene inspector independently shows/hides each card and selects an existing published article or series. Hiding a card does not delete content. Picks reference slugs in the theme snapshot; they do not copy article or series bodies.

The selected article opens the existing reading panel; the selected series opens its existing chapter journey. Hidden, missing or unavailable content produces no empty clickable card. The original featured article and the demonstration AI series are migration defaults. Desktop cards balance the lower sides of the character; compact layouts place them together beneath the scene navigation. Theme draft and Apply continue to control publication of these card settings.

## Planned member recommendations

For signed-in members, recommend articles and series based on their reading interests and time spent, retaining the owner's curated picks as a fallback. Recommendations should replace the targets in these same cards rather than adding competing panels. Respect member privacy and make personalization controllable. Guest visitors see the owner's picks. These are future requirements: the prototype does not collect behavioral recommendation signals or implement a recommendation engine.

Production needs agreed consent/preferences, member identity, useful interaction signals, ranking rules and backend contracts. Keep recommendations separate from authored content and preserve the owner's visibility choices. Do not claim a personalized recommendation until a real member-specific result exists.
