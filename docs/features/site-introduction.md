# Public site introduction and membership

Status: planned. This is an informational public page, separate from the private owner Studio and from login/account screens.

## Purpose

Help visitors understand the site's writing, reading tools and future membership. The page should feel like part of the same expressive site while giving clear, calm explanations of what membership adds.

## Visitor-facing content

- Explain that members can keep a personal reading/work notebook: their notes and saved text highlights appear in their own account.
- Explain private saved-article collections: save an article, organize it under personal categories such as “Kişisel gelişim” or “Türk edebiyatı”, move/remove it later, and use “Kaydedilenler” when no category is chosen. Saving does not publish the member’s list or categories. The member-view frontend prototype exists; real account storage remains planned.
- Describe account-synced notes and automatic history of articles or series chapters they have visited, so they can return to them later.
- Explain that anonymous visitors can browse the public site; any local notes made without an account stay in that browser until they are deliberately imported or discarded.
- State clearly that a visit is browsing history, not proof that an article was finished or understood.
- Describe planned member benefits as available features only when the associated account and storage features are actually working. Until then, label them as planned and keep any join link informational or unavailable; never imply that an account has been created.

## Privacy and boundaries

Personal notes, highlights and history belong to the signed-in member. They are private by default and do not become public annotations. Public annotation sharing, if ever added, requires a separate explicit publish action. Moving browser-local notes into an account requires the visitor's choice.

This page does not expose owner-only Studio controls. The frontend-only demo sign-up and account flows now exist; real authentication, account storage and device synchronization still require backend contracts. V1 members cannot write posts or enter Studio.

## Design and acceptance

Use a concise page composition with clear sections such as "Oku", "Kendine sakla" and "Kaldığın yere dön". Keep promised benefits concrete, distinguish available from planned behavior, provide a keyboard-accessible route back to the site, and review the page on mobile and with reduced motion. Do not add a generic dashboard before membership flows exist.
